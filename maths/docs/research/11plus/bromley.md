 Applying for a school place

 You must apply for a secondary school place for your child to start Year 7 at secondary school when your child is in year 6 at primary/junior school.

 Applications for Year 7 places in September 2027 open on 1 September 2026

 The national closing date for applications is 31 October 2026

 The national offer date for on-time secondary applications is 1 March 2027

 If you are a Bromley resident, you need to make your application through London Borough of Bromley. You can name schools outside of Bromley on your application if you wish.

If your child is currently at an independent school or a school outside Bromley and you wish to apply for a maintained secondary school, you must apply in the same way.

 If you live outside of Bromley, you need to apply through the local authority where you live and can name Bromley schools on that application if you wish.

 If you are looking to apply for a Year 7 place in the school year starting September 2026 you need to complete a paper application form. To obtain a form, please email secondary.admissions@bromley.gov.uk .

 If your child is already at secondary school, and you wish them to move to a different school you must apply as an in-year admission .

 Making an application

 Bromley strongly encourage families to apply online.

 Please ensure that all required supporting documents are provided with your application to ensure that it can be processed without delay. Please see application terms and conditions for further information.

 Bromley residents should apply online.

 eadmissions.org.uk 

 There are various pieces of useful information and guidance videos available on the eAdmissions website to help you.

 Bromley Admissions process all school applications for London Borough of Bromley residents. You cannot apply direct to schools. If you live outside the London Borough of Bromley, you must apply via your home Local Authority/Council, and you can include Bromley schools on your application.

 Further information

 You can download the applying for a secondary school place 2027-28 booklet for more information and advice.

 Secondary education in Bromley 2026/2027 

 Some schools may require you to complete an additional form called a Supplementary Information Form (SIF) or Category Form. These are clearly marked in the above booklet. SIFs and Category Forms are available from the relevant school and must be returned to the school by the closing date for applications. A SIF or Category Form is not an application for a school place, and you must also apply for the school as detailed above.

 If you have any questions regarding the secondary admissions process, please feel free to email us and we will be happy to help you.

 Children with an Education, Health, and Care Plan (EHCP)

 If your child has an Education, Health, and Care Plan, you must not apply through this admissions process. You will need to speak to your SEN caseworker at the Council on 020 8313 4739 for advice on how to apply for secondary schools.

 Key Stage 4 transition - University Technical Colleges (UTCs)

 Whilst there are no UTCs in the London Borough of Bromley, students have the opportunity to apply for these in other areas if they wish.

 University Technical Colleges (UTC’s) are government funded schools with a science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM) focus.

 They provide a unique and relevant approach to education, which addresses the changing needs of student and employers in the 21st Century. They are established by companies and universities, provide sought-after technical qualifications and benefit from industry standard equipment and specialist staff to provide students with the skills valued by employers.

 Most UTCs admit from Year 10 and the application period is the same as the main secondary admission process.

 You can find details of the UTC’s available and what they offer at www.bakerdearing.org/supporting-utcs/find-a-utc/ .

## in-year admission (linked from the page above)

Apply to move schools

 Apply to move schools

 In year admissions are for any year group between Reception and Year 11 during the school year and outside of the normal transfer process for Reception, Year 3 (junior schools only) and Year 7.

 If your child is already at primary, infant, junior or secondary school and you want them to move to a different school during the school year, you need to apply for the school(s) you wish them to move to. This is called an in year admission.

 You need to apply for an in year admission if:

 you have moved into Bromley and want your child to attend a Bromley school

 you are a Bromley resident and want to move your child from their current school to a Bromley school

 your child is currently home educated, and you wish them now to attend a Bromley school

 You live outside of Bromley and wish to apply for a Bromley school

 You do not need to apply for an in year admission if your child is:

 in Year 2 at an infant school and due to move to Year 3 in a junior school in the next academic year. You need to apply for a primary school place .

 In Year 6 at primary or junior school and due to move to Year 7 at secondary school in the next academic year. You need to apply for a secondary school place 

 If your child has an Education, Health, and Care Plan (EHCP) you cannot apply through the mainstream admission processes. You will need to contact the Special Education Needs team on 020 8313 4739 for advice.

 The council co-ordinates the in year admission applications for all Bromley schools and academies, except for those listed below where you will need to apply directly:

 Bishop Justus Church of England School

 Harris Academy Beckenham

 Harris Girls’ Academy Bromley

 Harris Academy Orpington

 Kemnal Technology College

 If you are looking to apply for any school or academy outside of Bromley, you will need to contact that school or their local authority. You cannot apply through the London Borough of Bromley.

 There is no guarantee that your preferred school(s) will be able to offer a place to your child. You should not remove your child from their current school until you have secured a place at another school.

 Your child will be added to the waiting lists for your named preferences and the council will notify you, in writing, if a place can be offered. The waiting lists for the 2026 to 2027 academic year will close on 31 May 2027, after which time you will need to reapply to continue to be considered for a place in the following academic year.

 If you are a Bromley resident and your named preferences cannot offer a place, and your child is without a school place, the council will look to secure a place at an alternative Bromley school. If you live outside of Bromley and the Bromley schools you apply to cannot offer a place, the legal responsibility to secure a school place for your child then lies with the local authority where you live.

 If you are looking to move schools in years 10 and 11, please consider that the curriculum is often already set and may not be able to match your child’s current courses or exam awarding bodies. It is important that you research what options are available for you child at your preferred school(s).

 How to apply for an in year admission

 The in year admission application is not currently available online. To apply, please download and print the relevant application.

 From Reception to Year 6

 In year primary school application form and guidance notes 

 From Year 7 to Year 11

 In year secondary school application form and guidance notes 

 Alternatively, forms can be collected from the main reception at Bromley Civic Centre (Monday to Friday 8.30am to 5pm).
