About the Medway Test | Medway Test: 11+ | Medway Council 

 About the Medway Test

 If you would like your child to be considered for a Medway grammar school they will need to sit the Medway Test (Medway's 11+).

 The exception to this is the admissions policy for Chatham Grammar . They allow children who are assessed as suitable for grammar school through the Medway Test or Kent Test.

 A child can take the Medway Test whether they live in Medway or not.

 If you live in Medway but are considering grammar schools in another area, your child will need to sit the relevant test for the schools in the other area.

 Registration and placement

 Once your child is registered, they will sit the Medway Test and get their results before the closing date for secondary school applications.

 You must register your child for the Medway Test on time if you would like them to be considered for a grammar school in Medway. Your child cannot register or sit the Medway Test at a later date.

 Register for the Medway Test 

 We are unable to provide a test for a child living outside of the UK, this includes British embassies or consulates abroad. 

 A child cannot sit any of the test papers remotely or anywhere else in the UK. A child must sit their test in a Medway Council test venue in Medway. 

 Passing the Medway Test does not guarantee a place in a Medway grammar school. Like other schools, grammar schools can get more applications than they have places for. If this happens, the schools must use their oversubscription criteria to decide who can be offered a place. You can read the schools' oversubscription criteria for more information.

 Medway Test papers

 The Medway Test is made up of 3 papers:

 English

 Mathematics

 Reasoning

 There is a minimum of a 20 minute break between test papers.

 These tests are written specifically for Medway Council.

 Our test provider is GL Assessment. Any test materials found online or in retailers, other than on the GL website are not official and are not supported or endorsed by GL or Medway Council.

 Instructions including time reminders will be provided by the invigilator on the test day.

 A clock (analogue or digital) must be available, working and clearly visible in the test room. It's the test venue’s preference on the type of clock to be used. A test venue can use a countdown or timer alongside the clock. However, using both a countdown and timer alongside the clock is not permitted.

 Remark, resit or appeals

 There is no resit and there is no remarking of any Medway Test papers. There is no appeal process for a remark of any paper.

Register for the Medway Test | Medway Test: 11+ | Medway Council 

 Register for the Medway Test

 Registration for the Medway Test opens on Monday 18 May and closes on Friday 12 June 2026.

 Before you register

 Before you register you should speak to your child’s teacher or headteacher to see if the Medway Test and grammar school is right for them.

 If a child is not working at or above their expected levels for their age, we do not recommend they sit the Medway Test.

 If you do not live in Medway

 You should think about whether you are likely to be offered a Medway grammar school even if your child is assessed as grammar. 

 If you do not live very close to Medway, you may not be able to get a place at a Medway school.

 Information about oversubscription and the furthest distance offered at each school is available in our school information pages .

 See our Medway Test information for children who do not live in Medway .

 Register your child

 You must provide your child's current school on your registration. If you cannot find it on the list, please email admissions@medway.gov.uk giving the name and address of the school. We'll add the school to the system so you can continue your registration.

 A test registration is not a school application. You must apply for schools separately during the September to October application window.

 If you have more than one child, for example twins or triplets in the same year group, you'll need to submit a separate registration for each child.

 For help with the registration process, read our Medway Test registration guide .

 Register for the Medway Test 

 After you have registered

 Check you have received a confirmation email saying your child is registered for the Medway Test. If you do not receive this email, your child is not registered.

 You can also view our familiarisation booklet to help your child become familiar with the test. The booklet will help your child familiarise themselves with the format and layout of the tests.

 Download the Medway Test Familiarisation booklet 

 There are accessibility issues with this document, for further help, email medwaytest@medway.gov.uk .

 Please use our libraries or community hubs if you want to print a copy.

 Registration for children with an education, health and care plan (EHCP)

 If your child has an EHCP they can still sit the Medway Test. You'll need to register in the same way as other families.

 Special arrangements

 If your child needs special arrangements to sit the test, you'll first need to register them for the test in the same way as other families.

 You must also speak to your child’s school before the registration closing date if you think your child needs special arrangements for the Medway Test. Their current school must submit a request by completing our special arrangements request form. The deadline for your school to submit a special arrangements request form is Monday 22 June 2026. The school must ask us for a copy of our special arrangements form and submit it on time.

 A child may need special arrangements if they have a disability or learning need.

 A special arrangements request must be submitted even if the only request is to sit the test on their own due to the child creating a distraction through movement or vocalisation, for example singing or humming without awareness whilst working.

 Special arrangements are not transferable between different local authorities and selection tests. If your child is granted special arrangements for one test, it does not mean they will be granted the same arrangements for a different test.

 Children with an EHCP or Person Centred Annual Reviews (PCAR) in completed or draft form must request special arrangements. They'll be granted 25% additional time where the school deems this appropriate. Other arrangements requested must be supported with evidence for consideration by panel.

 If your child is granted special arrangements, they might not sit the test with their peers or pupils from the same school.

 Your child will be allowed to take and use their asthma inhaler during the test day. You do not need to complete a request form for your child to use their inhaler. 

 There are no compensatory marks for the Medway Test. If you think your child requires arrangements to access the test because of an unexpected traumatic event, you must follow the steps set out in the special arrangements guidance.

 To find out more about special arrangements, see our special arrangements guide.

 Download the special arrangements guide 

 Late registration

 Registrations after the closing date cannot be accepted. There are no exceptions to this.

 Updating your child's details after registration

 If you need to update your child's details after registration, email your request to medwaytest@medway.gov.uk .

 We cannot guarantee any changes can be made in time for the test.

 Medway Test registration guide

 A step-by-step guide on how to register your child for the Medway Test.

 Late registrations after the closing date will not be accepted.

 1. Go to the admissions portal

 visit the admissions portal 

 press the Medway Test registration button.

 2. Create an account or log in

 If you have used the portal before, you can log in using your email address and password that you already set up and continue.

 If this is your first time using the portal:

 press create account

 enter your details

 press submit.

 You’ll be sent a verification email with a link.

 Follow the account setup instructions then go to the admissions portal and press the Medway Test registration button.

 3. Register for the Medway Test

 You must complete the registration.

 The questions include:

 Applicant details

 Check your details.

 If they're wrong, edit them and press save and continue.

 Select child

 If the child you want to register is listed, select them and press next.

 If the child you want to register is not listed:

 press new child

 enter their details

 press save

 select your child from the list

 press next.

 Select Admissions Round

 Select: Medway Test 2026 (September 2027 Admission)

 press next.

 read the information and the requirements for if you think your child needs special arrangements to sit the Medway Test.

 answer the questions

 Review and submit Medway Test registration

 review all the information on the page

 if any details are wrong, use the edit button next to the section to make changes, then press save and continue until you return to the review page.

Medway Test dates | Medway Test: 11+ | Medway Council 

 Medway Test dates

 The Medway Test (11+) is sat by children in year 6, before they go to secondary school.

 Medway Test invitations

 Invitations will be sent by the end of August 2026.

 If you gave us a valid email address when you registered for the test, your invitation will be sent by email.

 Please check your junk or spam folders if you have not received your invitation. If you have not received your invitation by September, email medwaytest@medway.gov.uk .

 If you did not provide an email address, you'll receive your invitation by post.

 Your invitation will tell you where and when your child is due to sit the test. Your child must only attend the test at their allocated venue and on their allocated day.

 Multiple birth, such as twins or triplets, will sit their test on the same day. This is regardless even if one or more sibling has been granted special arrangements. This means that one or more sibling may test at a different test centre to each other. Plan your journey to ensure you are on time at multiple test centres.

 Where and when your child will sit the test

 Children who go to a Medway maintained primary or junior school

 If your child goes to a primary or junior school in Medway, they'll sit the test at their own school.

 The test will take place on Tuesday 15 and Wednesday 16 September 2026.

 Those testing with special arrangements may test on Thursday 17 and Friday 18 September 2026 instead, depending on what their primary school choose.

 Medway independent schools

 St Andrew’s school pupils will test Saturday 19 September 2026 at St Andrew’s School.

 King’s School Rochester pupils will test on either Saturday 19 or Sunday 20 September 2026 at an allocated test centre.

 Children who do not go to a Medway primary or junior school

 Children who do not go to a primary or junior school in Medway will test in a Medway Test Centre on either Saturday 19 or Sunday 20 September 2026. One day will be allocated.

 Important information about test dates

 Your child is allocated a test date by us rather than parental preference. There is no alternative test date, including where the Medway Test clashes with other tests. If the Medway Test clashes with a different test, you'll need to decide which test your child sits.

 Your child’s test date and venue cannot be swapped with another child at your request.

 The arranged test dates are your child’s only chance to sit the test. The test cannot be taken later.

 Test date information is correct at the time of publication but may be subject to change.
