 Visit the CCHS website for more about the entrance test for September 2027. 

 Essex grammar school places

 Entry to Essex grammar schools is based on the results of the entrance test. If your child took the test and was assessed as suitable, the Essex grammar school will consider your application.

A short guide to secondary school admissions 2027 – Secondary school admissions – Southend-on-Sea City Council 

 Secondary school admissions

 A short guide to secondary school admissions 2027

 If you live in Southend-on-Sea and have children in year 5 getting ready for the application process to year 7 in September 2027 this guide will help you.

 The secondary school admissions booklet is now available. This will give you all the details needed to apply.

 Applications can be made between 1 September 2026 and 31 October 2026.

 Secondary school open days

 All secondary schools will be holding open days where they will provide you with information about their school, for further information, please see the secondary school opens days page .

 What do I do when the applications open?

 When the applications open:

 keep a record of all your actions. For example, the username and password you use when you register online

 register and apply online between 1 September to 31 October. You can amend the application as many times as you need up to 31 October. You do not have to wait until you have the results of selection or aptitude tests

 remember to register for any other aptitude tests, for example The Eastwood Academy and complete any Supplementary Information Form for faith schools

 you can apply for schools in and out the city. We recommend that one of your choices is your catchment school

 we recommend that you use all 5 preferences when you apply

 You must apply to Southend-on-Sea City Council if you:

 live in the city

 pay council tax to Southend-on-Sea City Council

 This is regardless of which primary or junior school your child currently attends.

 What happens between after the closing date and national offer day, 1 March 2027?

 Applications are processed, addresses checked.

 Applications are ranked per school and checked.

 All offers are recorded and highest offer possible matched.

 Exchanges of files with other local authorities for schools outside the area and applicants applying from other local authorities.

 Offers are drafted and after multiple exchanges.

 Offers are prepared.

 Offers are finalised and outcomes ready in time for 1 March 2027.
