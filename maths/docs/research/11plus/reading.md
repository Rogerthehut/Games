 Welcome to the Transition area of our school website!

 Year 10–13 Trip to see As You Like It at Shakespeare's Globe Theatre
 Published 03/11/26
 On Friday 16 October after school, we had the wonderful opportunity to watch As You Like It performed at the Globe Theatre. Despite being a long addition to a school day, it was absolutely worth it to see the actors performing in the likeness of the Elizabethan period, and demonstrate their talent on stage. The Globe itself was smaller than I had expected. However, this helped the actors to interact with the audience and raise the atmosphere of the entire performance. Even though the original play script is not a musical, there was a lot of music throughout the play and the audience was involved in a lot of the singing as well, which was a delight. As a GCSE Art student, the creation of the set throughout the play was extremely admirable, done in short periods of time to allow the actors the time to switch costumes and characters backstage while changing the set for the benefit of the audience’s understanding. We were fortunate enough to be seated lower down and quite close to the stage, allowing for close interactions with the performers (they definitely smiled at us during the ending bows). Overall, it was an amazing experience and I hope to be able to go again, whether with the school or in my own time. – Ishita , Year 11

 Royal Air Squadron Trophy
 Published 14/07/26
 After months of preparation, the Air Squadron Trophy (AST) team, made up of a group of RAF Cadets from both Kendrick and Reading School, secured 3rd place nationally, competing against a multitude of schools all across the country. 

 The Royal Air Squadron Trophy, or RAST, is a competition where a group of 13 cadets, part of the Combined Cadet Forces, take part in a series of events, ranging from Drill to Air Rifle Shooting to First Aid. Reading School CCF have been taking part in these events for almost a decade, and we performed exceptionally this year, both in the regional and national competitions.

 Examinations

 At present the school uses the following examination boards for GCSE, AS and A2 examinations: AQA, EDEXCEL, OCR and Eduqas.

Parents are requested to contact the school at the earliest opportunity to ensure appropriate arrangements are made both in curriculum provision and in public examinations.
