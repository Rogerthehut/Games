 Secondary school tests, assessments and supplementary forms

 For starting Secondary School at year 7 in September 2027
 Some schools will need your child to sit a test or be assessed for a place. 
 If you complete a supplementary/additional form for any school, you must also name it as one of your preferences on your application if you want to be considered for a place at the school. Completing a supplementary/additional form alone is not a valid application, and you will not be considered for a place at the school unless it has also been named as a preference in your application.
 For the Selective Eligibility Test you'll find out the results before the closing date for applications. If your child is eligible for a place, you can put that school on your application.
 The table below informs you when you have to register for these tests. Contact the school to find out more and register.
 After you've registered, the school will send you invitations to tests or assessments.
 Success in tests or assessments for any of these schools does not guarantee an offer of a place.
 Please ensure you verify these dates via the school's own website.

 To register, go to the website of any of the schools. You only need to register your child once.
 After you have registered, the schools will send invitations to tests or assessments.
 Success in the selective eligibility test means:
 Greenshaw High School – eligible to be considered for an ability place.
 Sutton Grammar School, Wallington County Grammar School, Wallington High School for Girls, Nonsuch High School for Girls and Wilson’s School – eligible to sit second stage entrance examinations.
 Supplementary forms
 Some schools may ask for a supplementary form with your application. For example, a faith school might need a reference from a parish priest. The deadline for both is 31 October.
 In Sutton, the schools that require Admission Administration document and a Certificate of Catholic Practise to be provided:
 St Philomena’s Catholic High School for Girls 
 The John Fisher School 
 Those of other faiths should refer to the Schools Admissions Policy for requirements and provide relevant evidence of their faith by 31 October.
 If you are applying for any school outside of Sutton, you should contact either the school or the Local Authority in which it is situated to see if a supplementary form is necessary.
