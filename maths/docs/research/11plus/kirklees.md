 The majority of places are taken up by students who live outside of the HGS catchment area. In a typical year, over three-quarters of places are offered to children from outside the catchment area. Students who live within the catchment area have to pass the entrance exam to exactly the same standard as other students, but if the school is oversubscribed, places will be offered to catchment area students as a priority.

 Exam Results

 We are proud to share the results our students achieved in their GCSE exams at the end of Year 11 in the most recent academic year.

 Key Stage 4 Results

 2025 

 Students are once again to be congratulated for their outstanding successes at GCSE. 84% of grades were at 7 or above, and of these 43% were grade 9.

 As well as some overall excellent results for the school, with 100% of our Year 11 students securing at least five good GCSE grades, 91% of our students achieved five or more GCSEs at grade 7 or above. In addition, 78% of our Year 11 students secured an impressive eight or more GCSEs at grade 7 or above.

 Some impressive overall statistics include:

 766 grades awarded were grade 9.

 116 students achieved ten or more GCSE at grades 7-9

 160 students achieved eight or more GCSEs at grades 7-9

 187 students achieved five or more GCSEs at grades 7-9.

 Further information about our performance in relation to other similar schools can be found on the Department for Education’s Compare School’s Performance website.
