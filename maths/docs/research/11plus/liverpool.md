 Year 7

 The Blue Coat School is a mixed selective school for students aged 11 – 18, which converted to Academy status on 1 st March 2012. We are over 300 years old, so we are rich in tradition, yet we are forward-looking in ideas and technology. Whilst we have a reputation for Academic results, there are many events and activities which make The Liverpool Blue Coat School experience very exciting and rewarding.

 September 2027 Admissions 

 At The Blue Coat School, Liverpool, we select students for Year 7 through an Entrance Test process. 

 The Phase 2 Entrance Test for September 2027 entry took place on Saturday 19 September 2026 and parents received the results on Friday 16 October 2026.

 September 2028 Admissions 

 Open Day 

 Parents and pupils considering an application for Year 7 entry in September 2028 are warmly invited to attend our Open Day on Thursday 25 March 2027.

 This event provides an opportunity to:

 Tour the school and our facilities 

 Meet staff and current students 

 Learn about the curriculum and wider school life 

 Further information regarding the admissions process for September 2028 entry, including Open Day booking arrangements, application details and key dates, will be published on this website in due course. Tickets for the Open Day will be available to book early in 2027.

 Admissions Arrangements 

 Admissions arrangements for September 2028 entry are currently being finalised. Full details, including application dates and assessment arrangements, will be published on this website as soon as they are available.

 Appeals Information 

 Timetable for September 2027 Entry to Secondary Schools 

 Appeals for September 2027 admission will be heard according to the following timetable:

 Offer Date 
 Appeals Lodged By 
 Appeals Heard By 

 1 March 2027 
 12 April 2027 
 15 June 2027 

 Parents are encouraged to submit appeals by the stated deadline. This helps ensure that appeals are heard and determined before the end of the summer term. Appeals submitted after the deadline may not be heard until September or October. Appeal hearings can only take place during school term time, as required by the School Admissions Appeals Code 2022. Appeals received by the deadline will be given priority.

 For further information regarding appeals, please contact: schoolappeals@wirral.gov.uk.
