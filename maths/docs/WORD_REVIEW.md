# Word lists for review

For a primary teacher to check. Mark anything ambiguous, too hard or too easy for the year shown, or not suitable. All of this is original and lives in `src/curriculum/reasoning.ts` (groups, links) and `src/curriculum/word-data.ts` (generated).

## Odd one out: word groups

A puzzle shows four words from one group and one from another. The odd word must belong to only one group.

- **mammals** (an animal): dog, cat, horse, cow, sheep, rabbit, fox, deer, mouse, goat, pig
- **birds** (a bird): robin, eagle, owl, sparrow, swan, parrot, crow, duck, hen, goose
- **fish** (a fish): salmon, trout, cod, haddock, tuna, carp, eel
- **insects** (an insect): ant, bee, wasp, beetle, fly, moth, ladybird
- **fruit** (a fruit): apple, pear, plum, grape, peach, melon, cherry, lemon, banana
- **vegetables** (a vegetable): carrot, leek, pea, onion, cabbage, turnip, celery, parsnip, sprout
- **colours** (a colour): red, blue, green, pink, grey, brown, yellow, purple
- **clothes** (something you wear): coat, hat, scarf, glove, shirt, sock, skirt, jumper
- **tools** (a tool): hammer, saw, drill, spanner, chisel, pliers
- **furniture** (a piece of furniture): chair, table, sofa, desk, shelf, stool, bed
- **vehicles** (a vehicle): bus, train, tram, lorry, taxi, van, car
- **body** (a part of the body): arm, leg, knee, elbow, wrist, ankle, hand, foot
- **instruments** (a musical instrument): drum, flute, harp, piano, violin, oboe
- **weather** (a kind of weather): rain, snow, wind, fog, hail, frost
- **sports** (a sport): rugby, tennis, golf, hockey, netball, boxing
- **countries** (a country): france, spain, italy, japan, chile, kenya
- **buildings** (a building): school, church, castle, library, shop, hospital
- **trees** (a tree): oak, ash, elm, pine, birch, willow, beech
- **flowers** (a flower): rose, tulip, daisy, lily, poppy, daffodil
- **metals** (a metal): iron, copper, tin, zinc, aluminium
- **drinks** (a drink): tea, milk, juice, water, coffee
- **rooms** (a room in a house): kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, lounge, attic, cellar
- **jobs** (a job): doctor, teacher, baker, farmer, nurse, plumber, pilot
- **planets** (a planet): mars, venus, saturn, jupiter, mercury, neptune
- **shapes** (a shape): circle, square, triangle, oval, pentagon, hexagon
- **toys** (a toy): doll, kite, ball, puzzle, robot, teddy
- **stationery** (something you write or draw with): pencil, ruler, rubber, eraser, crayon, marker

Pairs used for harder puzzles (neighbouring ideas): mammals/birds, birds/insects, fruit/vegetables, mammals/fish, tools/furniture, instruments/sports, vehicles/buildings, trees/flowers, rooms/furniture, jobs/tools.

Pairs never mixed: fruit/colours, flowers/colours, fruit/drinks, metals/tools, planets/countries, shapes/buildings, body/clothes, toys/stationery, rooms/buildings.
