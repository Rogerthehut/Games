# Recognised qualifications

## Route to pilot

Keep the existing in-app award labelled **Quarter pathway certificate**. It records a pass on Quarter's own assessment. A regulated qualification needs its own specification, assessment process and awarding organisation.

| Market | Candidate route | Decision to obtain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| England | GCSE Mathematics through an approved exam centre, with AQA as an initial discovery candidate. Consider Functional Skills mathematics separately where the learner and destination are a fit. | Exact qualification number, age and entry rules, tier, private-candidate arrangements, access arrangements, fees, dates and who issues the certificate. |
| Wales | Map Curriculum for Wales against its own progression steps. Explore the WJEC GCSE Mathematics and Numeracy Double Award through a centre. Consider Essential Skills Wales Application of Number separately for the appropriate audience. | Confirm listing and availability in Wales, centre responsibilities, assessment languages, age and entry rules, reasonable adjustments, and any limits on how Quarter can describe the offer. |
| Scotland and Northern Ireland | Treat each as a separate curriculum and qualification discovery exercise. Start with the relevant national regulator and approved provider for the target award. | Confirm the exact qualification and who accepts it before building the pathway. |
| Outside the UK | Evaluate each destination separately. Explore a named international qualification and its centre network if the audience warrants it. | Obtain written confirmation of local entry, delivery, recognition and any equivalence needed for the learner's intended use. |

## Work through the options

1. **Preparation and referral.** Map Quarter to an existing specification. A partner centre handles candidate entry and assessment under its awarding organisation's rules. This is the first route to test.
2. **Approved centre or delivery partner.** Ask the awarding organisation what roles Quarter may perform and what approvals, facilities, staff and audit evidence would be needed. Use written agreements before opening enrolment or advertising a regulated outcome.
3. **Own qualification.** Consider direct awarding-organisation recognition only once there is a business case and capacity for ongoing governance, qualification design, standard setting, appeals, malpractice handling and regulatory reporting. Qualification approval or accreditation requirements are a separate question from recognising the awarding organisation.

## Evidence for a partner pilot

- Provide a versioned content map, sample assessment blueprint, original sample items, marking criteria and accessibility examples.
- Agree a responsibility matrix covering entry, identity, invigilation, assessment security, marking, moderation, appeals, complaints, safeguarding, records, data retention and certificate issue.
- Verify the exact regulated qualification and awarding organisation against the relevant register. Confirm permission for names, logos and recognition claims in writing.
- Test with a small cohort once agreements, accessibility arrangements and safeguarding responsibilities are in place. Review the outcomes with the centre before expanding.
- Track dates, fees, minimum cohort, delivery method and feedback for each candidate. Public source research is complete for the initial shortlist. Partner conversations, approvals, a regulated pilot and certificate issuance are pending.
